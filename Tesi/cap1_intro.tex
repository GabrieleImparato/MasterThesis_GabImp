In the field of industrial warehouse storage, one of the main challenges is the full automation of the processes carried out within a facility, ranging from mission scheduling to the determination of the optimal routes to be followed inside the warehouse.

When a warehouse is designed and built from scratch, introducing automated systems is generally easier, since the layout and infrastructure can be specifically planned to accommodate them. Conversely, automating an existing facility is considerably more challenging, particularly when Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) need to operate in environments that were originally designed for manually operated forklifts and human workers.

In such cases, one possible approach is to redesign the operational workflow and use simulation and optimization algorithms to determine how available resources, such as AGVs, forklifts, and human operators, should be allocated and coordinated.
